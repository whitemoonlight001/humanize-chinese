Foundation pit construction in urban areas is often situated in close proximity to existing buildings and underground pipelines, necessitating extremely stringent deformation control measures. Consequently, achieving an optimal balance between structural safety and economic efficiency for support systems under complex geological conditions and rigorous environmental constraints has emerged as a central challenge in deep foundation pit engineering design. This study examines a deep foundation pit project for a two-level underground island platform station, proposing distinct support schemes for the standard section and the end section. The standard section employs an 800 mm thick diaphragm wall integrated with a hybrid support system comprising an initial concrete strut followed by three subsequent steel pipe struts. This configuration leverages the combined soil retention and waterproofing capabilities of the diaphragm wall alongside the rapid construction and material recyclability advantages of steel struts. For the end section, supplementary in-pit soil reinforcement is implemented in addition to the hybrid support system to address insufficient soft soil shear strength and mitigate time-dependent creep deformation. Internal force and deformation calculations, together with stability verifications。 are conducted using the incremental method and the Swedish slice method. These analytical approaches are complemented by an information-based construction monitoring system that tracks deep horizontal displacement, surface settlement, and support axial forces. Computational and monitoring results demonstrate that both the maximum wall displacement and all stability parameters for the standard section and end section satisfy the control criteria for first-class foundation pits. The selected support scheme exhibits excellent performance in deformation suppression and construction efficiency, thereby validating the applicability of the diaphragm wall combined with a hybrid support system for ultra-deep and environmentally sensitive foundation pits. These findings provide valuable technical references for similar engineering applications. 我国城市轨道交通推进迅速，预计到2026年末，内地开通城轨运营的城市将超过50座，运营总里程突破1.5万公里，其中地铁占比稳定在八成以上。高密度线网格局下，地铁车站作为关键节点，其工程质量直接决定系统运行的可靠性。车站施工难以避免深基坑难题。此类基坑开挖深、周边氛围拥挤、地层条件不均。市区基坑常紧邻既有建筑、地下管廊和交通干道，一旦地层位移控制失当，邻近设施将率先受到损害。这几年，深基坑领域涌现出不少新技术，如装配式地连墙、预应力鱼腹梁支撑体系、数字化监测系统等，不同程度地提升了施工安全冗余和效率。但不同城市地质背景与边界条件差异显著，直接套用既有方案往往不可行，设计工作仍需回归具体场地条件做细致推敲和调整。1.2　研究意义 处置得当的深基坑方案，除避免自身失稳外，能更有效地保护邻近建筑和地下管线，降低工程风险。从工程层面看，设计合理性先说体现为基坑整体稳定—坍塌或过量变形的风险被压缩在可控范围内，保证主体结构施工节奏，同样，支护参数优化直接关系材料用量与造价，具有明确的经济意义。从社会维度看。市区深基坑施工常伴随扬尘、噪声、交通改道等干扰，影响居民生活。这些扰动的程度与设计阶段的预判及缓冲措施密切相关，考虑周全的方案可从源头削减负面效应。在学术训练层面，毕业设计是对深基坑理论与具体工程条件的完整对接演练，过程中积累的判断方式与分析习惯，对日后实务工作带有铺垫价值，还有，本设计给出的可比选工况与参数选取思路，或可为同类项目带来一定参照。1.3　探究内容
